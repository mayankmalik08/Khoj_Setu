## About KhojSetu

KhojSetu is a digital discovery and comparison marketplace designed to connect customers with local shops, retailers, wholesalers, manufacturers, distributors and service providers.

Many small businesses have valuable products and services but lack the digital visibility needed to reach new customers. At the same time, customers often struggle to discover suitable businesses and compare important details such as pricing, quality specifications, minimum order quantities and delivery timelines.

KhojSetu aims to bridge this gap through one accessible digital platform.

**Discover businesses. Compare options. Connect directly.**

## The Problem

### For customers
- Finding suitable local businesses can be difficult.
- Comparing prices often requires contacting multiple sellers separately.
- Important details such as MOQ, availability and delivery time may not be easily accessible.
- Buyers may have limited information about business credibility and capabilities.

### For small businesses
- Many businesses lack a dedicated website or digital storefront.
- Customer acquisition often depends on referrals and walk-in traffic.
- It can be difficult to showcase products, services and capabilities online.
- Smaller businesses may struggle to compete for digital visibility.

## Our Solution

KhojSetu provides a common platform where customers can discover businesses, compare relevant offerings and send direct enquiries.

Businesses can create digital profiles, showcase their products and services, and receive enquiries from potential customers.

### Core Features

**1. Business Discovery**

Search and explore local shops, manufacturers, wholesalers and service providers by category, product, service and location.

**2. Smart Comparison**

Compare relevant details such as:
- Price
- Product specifications
- Quality information
- Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)
- Delivery time
- Availability
- Customisation
- Ratings and reviews

**3. Digital Business Storefronts**

Give small businesses an online presence where they can display their business information, products, services, images and capabilities.

**4. Direct Enquiries and Quotations**

Allow customers to contact individual businesses or request quotations from multiple selected suppliers.

**5. KhojSetu ChoiceLoop**

An optional one-question feedback feature that asks customers why they selected a particular business.

Possible responses include:
- Better price
- Better quality
- Faster delivery
- Lower MOQ
- Better ratings or trust
- Other

Aggregated responses can help businesses understand customer preferences and identify areas for improvement.

Individual customer responses are not intended to be exposed to businesses.

**6. AI-Assisted Business Onboarding**

A guided onboarding experience designed to help small business owners create structured business listings through simple questions.

## How It Works

### Customer Journey

1. Search for a product or service.
2. Discover relevant businesses.
3. Compare their offerings.
4. Send enquiries or request quotations.
5. Select a suitable business.
6. Optionally share feedback through ChoiceLoop.

### Business Journey

1. Register on KhojSetu.
2. Create a business profile.
3. Add products and services.
4. Receive customer enquiries.
5. Respond with quotations.
6. Understand aggregated customer decision insights.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Node.js
- npm
- Git

### Installation

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/mayankmalik08/Khoj_Setu.git
```

Move into the project directory:

```bash
cd Khoj_Setu
```

Install dependencies:

```bash
npm install
```

Start the development server:

```bash
npm run dev
```

The local development URL will be shown in your terminal.

*If your project uses different scripts or a different directory structure, follow the package.json configuration.*
